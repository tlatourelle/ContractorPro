# Customer Discovery — Questions to Ask

Use these in informal conversations with **small residential GCs**, and optionally **subs** and **homeowners**. Log answers in [discovery-log.md](./discovery-log.md).

**Goal:** Validate wedge, cascade feature, messaging, pricing sensitivity, and tool stack before writing a PRD.

---

## Screening (GC)

1. How many employees? How many active jobs right now?
2. Residential, commercial, or mix? New construction, remodel, or both?
3. What software do you use today for scheduling, client communication, and books?
4. What do you pay per month for software (total)?

---

## Pain & workflow

5. Walk me through what happens when **one trade slips** (e.g., framing delayed 3 days). Who do you tell? How?
6. How do **homeowners** find out about delays today? What goes wrong?
7. How do you coordinate **subs** — text, email, phone, group chat, something else?
8. What’s the most annoying repeat call/text you get from homeowners or subs?
9. Do you use **Google Calendar** for job scheduling? Do subs and homeowners use it too, or mostly text/calls?
10. Does anyone on your team rely on **Outlook / Microsoft calendar** for jobs, or only for office email?
11. Have you tried BuilderTrend, Contractor Foreman, Jobber, or similar? Why stay or leave?
12. If you demo'd Buildertrend — what killed it? Price, setup time, or getting subs on board?
13. When a date moves, do you update Google Calendar, your PM tool, or both?
14. Would you pay for schedule sync + sub texts **without** estimating/invoicing built in?
15. Weekly summary vs instant "here's what changed" when you drag a date — which saves more phone tag?
16. How long did onboarding take for your last construction software? Would 10 minutes to first invite matter?

---

## Cascade feature (validate)

12. If moving “drywall start” automatically shifted everything after it, would you use that every time or only sometimes?
13. What should **not** auto-move? (inspections, homeowner selections, material lead times?)
14. Would you want to **preview** who gets notified before confirming a schedule change?
15. Business days only, or calendar days?

---

## Homeowner & sub experience

16. Would your homeowners open a **web link** for schedule updates? What would make them ignore it?
17. Is **SMS** enough for “your tile install moved to March 20” with a link for details?
18. If we gave you a **project phone number** and subs/homeowners texted it (like adding someone to a group chat) — and everything logged to the project — would you **switch** from your current text thread or run both?
19. Do you use **one group text per job** or separate threads per sub/homeowner?
20. Should subs see **only their tasks** or more of the job timeline?
21. Should subs **confirm dates** with a one-tap ack, or is view-only enough?
22. Should homeowners ever message subs directly, or always through you?

---

## Willingness to pay

22. If this saved you 2 hours/week of phone tag, what’s a fair **monthly price**?
23. What would a **free tier** need to include for you to try it? What would make you upgrade?
24. Would you pay more for **AI-drafted** homeowner updates you edit and send?

---

## Integrations

25. Do you use **QuickBooks Online**? What would a “good enough” link look like?
26. Anything else that must connect (email, CompanyCam, supplier portals)?

---

## For subs (optional)

1. How do GCs usually tell you schedule changes today?
2. Would a simple link showing **your dates only** be useful?
3. Would you pay for this? (expect: no — confirms GC is buyer)
4. If a GC texted you a link, would you join with **just your name and phone** — no password, no app?
5. For return visits, would you rather get a **new link in each text** or use **one bookmark**?

---

## For homeowners (optional)

1. How do you prefer updates from your contractor — call, text, email, portal?
2. Would you **send photos** to your contractor (issues, questions)? How do you do that today?
3. Would you check a **schedule link** during a remodel? What would you want to see?
4. Would you join a project portal with **name + phone only** — no account setup?
5. Is a **6-digit phone verification code** on first join acceptable?

---

## After each interview

Record in `discovery-log.md`:

- Name / role / company size (or anonymous ID)
- Top 3 pains quoted verbatim
- Current tools + monthly spend
- Reaction to cascade + magic link + SMS + SMS relay
- Price anchor they mentioned
- Surprises / contradictions to our assumptions
