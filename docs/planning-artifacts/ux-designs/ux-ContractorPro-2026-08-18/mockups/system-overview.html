Outside services (vendors)

Twilio Company text number

One Twilio line per company (e.g. Acme Co). All subs/customers text it. Staff get SMS relays; app inbox mirrors threads. ~$1.15/mo + message volume — not one number per job.

Google Calendar + Drive + sign-in

Calendar sync on sub accept. Drive folder per job for plans/photos (subs see files via app QR portal, not drive.google.com). Optional Google sign-in for your team.

Email provider Magic links & invites

Sends passwordless links to subs and customers (confirm dates, join project). TBD vendor (e.g. Resend, SendGrid).

How it fits together

Plan → Prelim → Finalize

Build the job schedule internally. Publish prelim for customer preview. Finalize to start sub cascade — only then do texts and calendar writes begin.

Talk — company # + inbox

Everyone texts Acme Co. You & Maci see the same threads in the app or via SMS relay. Reply to Acme # so it's logged.

Commit — schedule in app

When dates move, change the schedule in ContractorPro. Confirm links go out via SMS/email (magic link — not "reply YES").

Job site — QR portal

Laminated QR → subs check in, view plans, upload end-of-day photos to the job Drive folder through the app.

What we are NOT building in v0.1

No native iPhone/Android app. No per-project text numbers or group MMS setup. Personal cell chats stay outside the system. Customer approval gate before subs (2B) — TBD with SMEs.
