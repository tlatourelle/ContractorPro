Two lanes in plain English

Lane 1 — Conversation (phone)

You create a group text per sub or customer: you + them + the project handle #. Everyone texts normally. ContractorPro logs the thread. Photos in text are saved to the project.

Lane 2 — Scheduling (web app)

When dates move, you change the schedule in ContractorPro — not by texting back and forth. The app sends confirm links via text/email. Subs and customers tap to accept.

What we are NOT building in v0.1

No native iPhone/Android app for you or subs. No replacing your personal cell number. No injecting into old group texts — you start a new group with the project handle. No AI reading texts to auto-reschedule (you commit changes in the app).

Facilitator note: This is the planning architecture for SME validation — vendors and hosting may change slightly before build, but the two-lane pattern (MMS for talk, app for schedule) is the product bet. Technical detail: project-handle-numbers.md, monthly-run-rate.md.
